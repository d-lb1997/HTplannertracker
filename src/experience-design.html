Standard versus tailored (light touch)

A supporting thread, not the centre of Discovery. The main outputs are the validated backlog, personas, IA, prototype, usability findings and branding. Here you only note how far HT expects to go beyond standard Slalom Connect! (Connect!, Plus, Plus+), so the Solution Architect can make the OOTB-versus-custom recommendation. Spend about ten minutes per source, not a workshop.

Watch the gap. HT’s business case recommends Option B (modern digital workplace) first. Slalom’s pitch already shows kiosks, a voice channel and AI shift briefings. Those are Slalom ideas, not confirmed HT asks. Test them as needs and keep them in a separate lane in the backlog.
